A healthier way to think about AI

AI is not the work. It helps you get back to the work.

Your business does not exist so you can sit in front of a screen all day. It exists to serve customers, solve problems, lead people, create value, and do the work only humans can do.

Used badly, AI becomes another distraction. Used well, it helps clear the clutter so you can move faster and spend less time buried in the tasks that pull you away from the work that matters.
